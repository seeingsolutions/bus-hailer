Hail a bus

Wording

Favourite buses

    Stores each favourite on the sign, so the button on its cable switches between them without the phone. With several stored, the sign cycles through them until you press the cable button.

    When you send from the phone

    "Show only this message" holds your message steadily but clears the stored favourites. "Keep stored favourites" adds your message after them, so it takes its turn in the cycle.

    Any message

    Long messages scroll across the sign automatically.

    Sign controls

    Colour
    Movement

    Automatic keeps short text steady and scrolls text that is too wide for the sign.

    Applies the next time you send. 5 was the old Normal and 10 was Fast.

    Setup and troubleshooting

    Close the CoolLED1248 app first. The sign only accepts one connection at a time.

    Six digits. New signs use 000000.

    Letter size
    Pixel layout

    Only change this if letters come out scrambled, upside down or split. Send the test pattern after each change.

    Preset hold time

    With several favourites stored, the sign cycles through them by itself. This sets how long it stays on each one. It applies the next time you save favourites.

    Which way up

    Double-tapping the button on the sign's cable also flips it.

    Turn this on if your sign doesn't appear in the list.
